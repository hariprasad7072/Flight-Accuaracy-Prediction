    About This System

    AeroPredict AI uses machine learning to predict flight delays with high accuracy. Our system analyzes 25 features including weather conditions, flight parameters, and operational metrics.

    Machine Learning Models: We train 6 different models (XGBoost, LightGBM, Random Forest, Neural Network, Voting Ensemble, Stacking Classifier) and use the best performer for predictions.

    Explainability: Every prediction includes SHAP (Shapley Additive Explanations) values, showing exactly which factors influenced the result.

    Accuracy: Our best model achieves 98.51% accuracy on test data, with comprehensive evaluation across multiple metrics.
